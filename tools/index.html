Bus Gear Calc

A VW bus gear ratio calculator. See your speed and RPM in every gear before you spend money on a gearbox, a ring and pinion or new tires.

What it does

Pick a bus, then its gearbox, gears and tires. The calculator shows your road speed and engine RPM in each gear. Stack up as many setups as you like and compare them side by side.

It covers every VW bus from 1950 to 2013: Split Window, Bay Window, Vanagon and Syncro, including the Brazilian buses built through 2013. It also handles swapped buses, with custom engines, gearboxes and ratios.

Thinking about a taller fourth, a different ring and pinion, or bigger tires? Try it here first and see what it does to your cruising RPM.

What you get

  • Speed in each gear at any engine RPM, in mph or km/h
  • RPM at road speed, so you know your highway cruise
  • Speedometer error after a tire or gearing change
  • Hill climbs on real grades like the Grapevine, Cajon Pass and Donner Summit
  • Side-by-side compare for stock and modified setups
  • A shareable link that opens with your exact setups

Air-cooled VW won't start?

A step-by-step no-start guide for any carbureted VW with the air-cooled Type 1 engine: Beetle, Karmann Ghia, early bus, Thing, Baja or buggy. Answer one question at a time. It narrows the problem down from battery to spark to fuel to timing.

Start troubleshooting →

Opens in a new window.

How it works

Turn the key and tell it what happens. Does the engine crank normally, crank slowly, just click, or do nothing at all? Each answer leads to the next check, until you reach a likely cause and a fix.

It is written for every carbureted VW with the upright Type 1 engine: Beetle and Super Beetle, Karmann Ghia, split and bay buses with the 1600 or smaller (US buses through 1971, Brazilian Kombis until 2005), Thing, Baja and dune buggy. It assumes points ignition and a Solex carburetor, with notes where 6-volt cars, electronic ignition and early carbs differ. Specs are shown where you need them, with safety notes for the steps that need care.

What it checks

  • Battery and cables, including the gearbox ground strap
  • Starter and solenoid: clicks, slow cranking, no cranking
  • Spark: coil, points, condenser, rotor and cap
  • Fuel: pump, lines, accelerator pump and choke
  • Timing and the other usual suspects

Questions

FAQ

Is the Bus Gear Calc free?

Yes. Both tools are free and run in your browser. No sign-up.

Which VW buses does the gear calculator cover?

Every air-cooled and water-cooled VW bus from 1950 to 2013: Split Window, Bay Window (including the Brazilian buses built through 2013), Vanagon and Syncro. You can also add custom engines, gearboxes and ratios for swapped buses.

Can it tell me my RPM at highway speed?

Yes. Pick your bus, gearbox, ring and pinion and tire size, and it shows engine RPM at any road speed in every gear, plus your speedometer error.

My Beetle, Ghia or bus cranks but won't start. Where do I begin?

Open the no-start guide and answer the first question. If the engine cranks at normal speed, it checks for spark first, then fuel, then timing.

Which VWs does the no-start guide cover?

Any carbureted VW with the upright Type 1 engine: Beetle and Super Beetle, Karmann Ghia, Type 2 bus with the 1600 or smaller, Thing, Baja and dune buggy. Fuel-injected cars and the Type 4 engine (1972-on US buses, 411/412, 914) need different fuel checks. Check your own engine's specs before adjusting.
