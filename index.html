Cooling

Underbelly radiator

Cooling for 300+HP, tucked under the bus.

  • Full-size radiator with the A/C condenser stacked on top, first in the airflow
  • Built-in transmission and engine oil coolers
  • Whole stack enclosed in a bent stainless housing
  • Sealed scoop and road screen, with two low-profile fans pulling air down through the stack
Suspension

Bus “OEM+” A-Arm

Bolt-on Vanagon front suspension for the Bay, with an upgraded cooling pack.

  • Vanagon control arms and knuckles on a bolt-in subframe
  • Upright radiator and matched A/C condenser in the subframe
Engine

EZ36 flat six

A 3.6 liter Subaru six, built to live in the back of a Bay.

  • 256 hp and 247 lb-ft stock, about 280 hp and 270 lb-ft with headers and exhaust (our estimate)
  • Stainless headers into a 911-style dual outlet muffler
  • Clean mil-spec harness with a compact interface box
Gearbox

Subaru + RGB

Subaru gearboxes turned around for a rear-engine bus with Reversaru reduction gearboxes.

  • 4EAT four-speed automatic or 5MT five-speed manual
  • RGB at 1:1 or 1.19, so a 4.11 final drive stays 4.11 or becomes 4.89
  • Try your setup in the Bus Gear Calc
